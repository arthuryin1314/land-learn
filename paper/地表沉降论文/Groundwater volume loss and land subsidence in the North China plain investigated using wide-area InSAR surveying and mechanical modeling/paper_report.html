2. 研究区域2. Study area

  • 华北平原（NCP），包括北京、天津、河北、山东、河南五省市；面积 300,000 km²（占全国 3.1%），人口 3.39 亿（占 24.2%，截至 2019 年 12 月）。The North China Plain, including Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong and Henan; 300,000 km² (3.1% of China) and 339 million people (24.2%, as of December 2019). p.2
  • 自西向东分为山前平原、洪积（冲积）平原与滨海平原三个水文地质单元；主要含水层为山前浅层含水层与洪积平原深层含水层；年均降水 560 mm，集中于 6–9 月。Divided west–east into piedmont, flood and coastal plains; main aquifers are shallow piedmont and deep flood-plain aquifers; mean precipitation 560 mm, concentrated June–September. p.2–3
  • 作者描述：山前平原以弹性变形为主，洪积平原以塑性变形为主、压密基本不可逆；农业灌溉比例最高达 90%；2013–2022 年用水量增加 126%。Authors: piedmont plain dominated by elastic, flood plain by plastic (largely irreversible) deformation; irrigation up to 90% of groundwater use; water consumption rose 126% in 2013–2022. p.3
  • 区内断层/地裂缝众多（名称见表 S1）；1971–2015 年沉降漏斗面积超过 48,500 km²。Numerous faults/ground fissures (Table S1); the subsidence funnel area exceeded 48,500 km² during 1971–2015. p.2–3

4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. SBAS-InSAR：GAMMA 干涉 + 自编 MATLAB SBAS；BT=80 天、BP=250 m，每景与后两景组对；ESD 配准（约 10⁻³ 样本）；多视 20×4；AW3D30 去地形；扩展最小费用流解缠；相位闭合差改正解缠误差；线性回归去高程相关对流层；二次多项式去残余斜坡；最小二乘求 LOS 速率与时序。SBAS-InSAR: GAMMA interferometry + in-house MATLAB SBAS; BT=80 days, BP=250 m, each scene paired with the next two; ESD co-registration (~10⁻³ sample); 20×4 looks; AW3D30 topography removal; extended MCF unwrapping; phase-closure correction; elevation-dependent troposphere by linear regression; quadratic ramp removal; least-squares LOS rates and series. p.5
  2. 宽域网络平差：假设水平形变可忽略，LOS/cosθ 转垂向；在 M=8 个 frame、N=13 个重叠区中取平均时间相干 >0.85 的同名点，以重叠区差值绝对值之和最小为目标，建立 V=B·X+ε，用 Moore–Penrose 伪逆求各 frame 改正量；重叠点取平均后统一到同一稳定基准。Wide-area network adjustment: Horizontal motion neglected, LOS/cosθ to vertical; homologous points with mean temporal coherence >0.85 in N=13 overlaps of M=8 frames; minimise the sum of absolute overlap differences via V=B·X+ε solved with the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse; overlaps averaged and tied to one stable benchmark. p.5–6
  3. 含水层参数 Ske：对 InSAR 与水位分别拟合“线性 + 单正弦”模型（T=0 对应 10 月 1 日），以季节形变振幅与季节水位振幅之比 Ske=Δd_s/Δh_s 计算弹性骨架储水系数；并用交叉小波（XWT）分析相位关系。Aquifer parameter Ske: Fit “linear + single sinusoid” models to InSAR and GWL (T=0 = 1 October); Ske=Δd_s/Δh_s from seasonal amplitudes; cross-wavelet transform (XWT) for phase relations. p.6–7, 10–11
  4. InSAR-VSM 地下水体积反演：基于均质各向同性弹性半空间格林函数（泊松比 0.25），把含水层离散为单元，观测权重 σ²∝1/相干²，以拉普拉斯平滑与 L 曲线选平滑因子；GWSL = n·h·l_c·w_c·Σdv。InSAR-VSM groundwater volume inversion: Elastic half-space Green’s functions (homogeneous, isotropic; Poisson’s ratio 0.25); aquifer discretised into cells; weights σ²∝1/coherence²; Laplacian smoothing with L-curve; GWSL = n·h·l_c·w_c·Σdv. p.7
  5. Zone 1 反演设置：多层含水层近似为单层；Zone 1 形变降采样到 1 km；100×150 个 2 km 单元（200 km × 300 km）；含水层厚 500 m；孔隙度 0.2–0.4；正演残差检验。Zone 1 inversion set-up: Multi-layer aquifer approximated as single layer; Zone 1 downsampled to 1 km; 100×150 cells of 2 km (200 km × 300 km); 500 m thickness; porosity 0.2–0.4; forward-model residual check. p.12–13
  6. 长期演变与驱动分析：三期水准 + GNSS + InSAR 构建 1960–2023 年 63 年演变；断层叠加分析；2021、2023 年强降雨事件响应；指数函数 def(t)=M(e^{kt}−1)（k∈[−1,0]）逐点拟合衰减系数 k，并对比南水北调分配。Long-term evolution and drivers: Leveling + GNSS + InSAR give a 63-year (1960–2023) history; fault overlay; response to 2021 and 2023 extreme rainfall; exponential def(t)=M(e^{kt}−1), k∈[−1,0], fitted pointwise and compared with SNWDP allocation. p.15–19

5. 创新点（作者自述）5. Innovations (author claims)

以下为作者在文中声称的贡献/新意，本笔记不对其作额外评价。Contributions claimed by the authors; no extra assessment is added.

  • 作者称首次以前所未有的高分辨率对整个华北平原地下水储量损失（GWSL）及其时空动态做全面定量评估。The authors claim the first comprehensive, quantitative assessment of GWSL and its dynamics across the entire NCP at unprecedented resolution. p.2
  • 结合宽域网络平差与空间约束，获得全平原无缝、毫米级精度的形变时序。Wide-area network adjustment with spatial constraints yields seamless, millimetre-level deformation series for the whole plain. p.1
  • 作者称把 InSAR 与体积应变模型（InSAR-VSM）结合构建“新的”地下水损失反演模型，得到首个 2 km 分辨率 GWSL 数据集。The authors claim a novel groundwater-loss inversion coupling InSAR with a volume strain model (InSAR-VSM), yielding the first 2-km GWSL dataset. p.1
  • 将近年形变放入 1960–2023 年 63 年背景，讨论人类活动（超采、南水北调、禁采）与自然因素（极端降雨、地质构造）的共同作用。Places recent deformation in a 63-year (1960–2023) context and discusses combined human (over-extraction, SNWDP, bans) and natural (extreme rainfall, structures) factors. p.1–2

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

沉降格局Subsidence pattern

  • P40 与 P142 的 LOS 沉降漏斗最大速率均超过 −120 mm/yr，其他局部漏斗超过 −100 mm/yr；P1–P3 点 3–9 月沉降、10 月至次年 2 月沉降减缓，与灌溉季节一致。Max LOS rates exceed −120 mm/yr on both P40 and P142, other local funnels exceed −100 mm/yr; points P1–P3 subside March–September and slow October–February, matching irrigation seasons. p.7
  • 两轨入射角相差近 20°，同位点 P2/P2′ 速率相对差约 10%；平差后轨间差异由双峰分布（均值 −4.3 mm/yr）变为近高斯单峰（均值 −2.1 mm/yr），跨轨“台阶”消除。Incidence angles differ by nearly 20°; co-located P2/P2′ rates differ by ~10%; after adjustment inter-track differences change from bimodal (mean −4.3 mm/yr) to near-Gaussian (mean −2.1 mm/yr), removing the step. p.7–8
  • 三个主要沉降区呈西南—东北向窄带：Zone 1 约 200 km × 500 km 农业灌溉区（作者称为世界最大连续沉降漏斗），Zone 2、Zone 3 分别位于天津与北京郊区。Three main zones form a SW–NE band: Zone 1, ~200 km × 500 km agricultural irrigation area (called by the authors the largest continuous funnel in the world); Zones 2 and 3 in the Tianjin and Beijing suburbs. p.10
  • 摘要与结论：最大垂向速率超过 −150 mm/yr，六年累计沉降可达 1 m。Abstract and conclusion: maximum vertical rate exceeds −150 mm/yr and cumulative subsidence reaches ~1 m over six years. p.1, 20

形变与水位响应Deformation–groundwater response

  • 12 口典型井（井深均 >178 m）去趋势后形变与水位高度一致，相关 ≥0.94；W8 井 XWT 显示一年主周期，相位差约 2°，即形变滞后水位约 2 天。At 12 typical wells (all >178 m deep), detrended deformation and GWL agree closely (r ≥0.94); XWT at W8 shows a one-year period with ~2° phase difference, i.e. a ~2-day lag. p.10–11
  • 洪积平原水位季节波动达 8–16 m，Ske 主要 <0.01，作者认为弹性恢复能力差、塑性变形占主导；山前平原 Ske 较高。Flood-plain seasonal GWL fluctuations reach 8–16 m with Ske mostly <0.01, interpreted as poor elastic recovery and plastic dominance; Ske is higher in the piedmont. p.11–12
  • 洪积平原季节形变峰值在 1–3 月，与灌溉主导的水位（1–3 月最高、6–7 月最低）一致。Flood-plain seasonal deformation peaks in January–March, matching irrigation-driven GWL (highest Jan–Mar, lowest Jun–Jul). p.12

地下水储量损失Groundwater storage loss

  • Zone 1 最大体积应变 Dvmax 为 −9.4×10⁻⁸；正演残差随机分布，最大 20 mm。Zone 1 maximum volumetric strain Dvmax is −9.4×10⁻⁸; forward residuals are random, max 20 mm. p.13
  • Zone 1（主要农业灌溉区）年地下水体积变化 −4.346 ~ −8.692 ×10⁸ m³/yr（孔隙度 0.2–0.4），损失集中于核心漏斗区。Zone 1 (main irrigation area) annual groundwater volume change −4.346 to −8.692 ×10⁸ m³/yr (porosity 0.2–0.4), concentrated in the core funnel. p.13
  • 与一维固结理论估算的 −1.26×10⁹ m³/yr 量级可比，作者视为交叉验证，并认为后者因忽略水平位移倾向高估。Comparable in order of magnitude with −1.26×10⁹ m³/yr from 1-D consolidation theory, which the authors treat as cross-validation and say tends to overestimate by neglecting horizontal displacement. p.13
  • InSAR 与 8 个 GNSS 点比较 RMSE 4.8–13.4 mm，R² 0.94–0.98。InSAR vs 8 GNSS points: RMSE 4.8–13.4 mm, R² 0.94–0.98. p.13

驱动因素与长期演变Drivers and long-term evolution

  • 沉降漏斗集中于与断层走向一致的南北向窄带，被太行山断裂与 BTK 断裂围限，作者认为这些断层可能起水力屏障作用；AY、ND 断层附近形变梯度高。Funnels concentrate in a narrow N–S belt parallel to fault strike, bounded by the Taihang Mountain and BTK faults, which the authors suggest may act as hydraulic barriers; deformation gradients are high near the AY and ND faults. p.15–16
  • “20210720”“20230729”两次百年一遇降雨期间各点沉降显著减缓甚至短暂反弹；洪积平原井水位平均上升 7.23 m 后很快回落；2019–2022 年水位下降 0.87 m/yr。During the “20210720” and “20230729” rainfall events subsidence slowed markedly or briefly rebounded; flood-plain GWL rose by 7.23 m on average then quickly returned; GWL declined 0.87 m/yr in 2019–2022. p.16–17
  • 1960–1980 年沉降主要在天津、沧州等城市；1990–2010 年农田沉降加剧；至 2010 年天津中心累计近 3 m、沧州 1.8 m、农田漏斗最大 2 m；天津 1960–2019 年线性沉降，2019–2020 年趋稳，2020 年 5 月后反弹。In 1960–1980 subsidence was mainly urban (Tianjin, Cangzhou); farmland subsidence intensified in 1990–2010; by 2010 Tianjin’s centre reached ~3 m, Cangzhou 1.8 m, farmland funnels up to 2 m; Tianjin subsided linearly 1960–2019, stabilised 2019–2020 and rebounded after May 2020. p.17
  • 以保定—沧州一线为界，北部（北京、天津）形变趋缓，南部（石家庄、邢台、邯郸）持续加剧；作者将差异归因于南水北调水量主要分配给京津城市工业用水，河北农业区分配密度仅 0.2。North of the Baoding–Cangzhou line (Beijing, Tianjin) deformation decreases; south (Shijiazhuang, Xingtai, Handan) it keeps increasing; the authors attribute this to SNWDP water going mainly to Beijing/Tianjin, with an allocation density of only 0.2 in Hebei’s farmland. p.18–20

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors

  • LOS 转垂向基于“水平形变可忽略”假设，在断层带等局部水平运动显著处可能引入不确定性；夏季作物生长导致相干性下降，影响部分区域数据质量。LOS-to-vertical relies on neglecting horizontal motion, which may add uncertainty near faults; summer crop growth lowers coherence and affects data quality in places. p.13
  • InSAR-VSM 把多层含水层近似为单层，可能无法刻画不同深度含水层的水力联系与差异响应。InSAR-VSM approximates the multi-layer aquifer as a single layer and may miss inter-layer hydraulic connections and differing responses. p.13, 15
  • 格林函数假设均质弹性介质，是对非均质地下的理想化；非弹性行为或复杂分层可能带来未被刻画的差异。Green’s functions assume a homogeneous elastic medium, an idealisation of the heterogeneous subsurface; inelastic behaviour or layering may cause unmodelled discrepancies. p.15
  • 泊松比 0.25 为经验值；孔隙度取 0.2–0.4 的区间直接影响 GWSL 估计。Poisson’s ratio 0.25 is empirical; the porosity range 0.2–0.4 directly affects GWSL. p.15
  • 2 km 网格是计算可行性与空间细节之间的折中，粗网格可能平滑局部特征。The 2 km grid trades computational feasibility against detail; coarse grids may smooth local features. p.15
  • 一维固结法所需的先期固结水头等历史数据在全平原极其稀缺，作者因此未采用该法作为主方法。Historical data such as preconsolidation head needed for the 1-D method are extremely scarce across the NCP, so it was not used as the main method. p.13

7.2 依据原文可核查的问题7.2 Issues verifiable in the text

每条均可在所注页码核对；只陈述事实，不推测影响。Each item can be checked on the cited page; facts only, no speculation.

  • GWSL 的空间范围：摘要与结论把 −4.346 ~ −8.692 ×10⁸ m³/yr 表述为华北平原（“the NCP”）年均地下水损失，而 5.5 节明确只对 Zone 1（主要农业灌溉区，2 km 网格 200 km × 300 km）计算。Spatial scope of GWSL: the abstract and conclusion present −4.346 to −8.692 ×10⁸ m³/yr as the NCP’s average loss, whereas Section 5.5 computes it only for Zone 1 (main irrigation area, 2 km grid of 200 km × 300 km). p.1, 12–13, 20
