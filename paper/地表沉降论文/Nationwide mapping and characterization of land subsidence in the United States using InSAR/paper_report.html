4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. 自适应 EDAD 改正：多项式去相位斜坡；把干涉图分为 6 km×6 km、50% 重叠的块，用 IRLS 拟合相位—高程关系；以质量指标 Q=σ/(kσh) 判断，Q>10 的块参数置零（即不改正）；块参数赋予中心像元后插值。Adaptive EDAD correction: Polynomial ramp removal; interferograms split into 6 km×6 km patches with 50% overlap; phase–elevation fitted by IRLS; quality Q=σ/(kσh), patches with Q>10 set to zero (no correction); parameters interpolated from patch centres. p.3
  2. 迭代 Stacking 与湍流大气：加权 Stacking 求平均速率；以初始速率标准差为阈值掩膜形变像元并膨胀，用非形变像元重估斜坡与 EDAD；再对稳定像元低通滤波估计湍流大气后重新 Stacking；AOI 另做 SBAS 时序。Iterative stacking and turbulent atmosphere: Weighted stacking for mean rates; deforming pixels (|v| > SD) masked and dilated, ramp/EDAD re-estimated on stable pixels; turbulent atmosphere from low-pass filtering of stable pixels, then re-stacking; SBAS time series for AOIs. p.3–4
  3. 区域网平差拼接：每个 frame 视为常数偏差，仅用 |速率| <1 cm/yr 的同名像元，最小二乘求 321 个 frame 偏差并改正；所有结果均保留在 LOS 方向。Block-adjustment mosaicking: A constant bias per frame estimated by least squares from homonymous pixels with |rate| <1 cm/yr, then removed for 321 frames; all results remain in LOS. p.4
  4. DeforNet 识别沉降区：基于 U-Net、加入注意力机制与深度可分离卷积；40 epoch、batch 8、初始学习率 0.0005（每 10 epoch 减半）、Adam；按州处理：低通去噪→缠绕为灰度→25% 重叠分块→识别→膨胀平滑→拼回；再用坡度 >5° 与地震目录剔除滑坡、地震形变。DeforNet extraction: U-Net with attention and depth-wise separable convolutions; 40 epochs, batch 8, initial LR 0.0005 halved every 10 epochs, Adam; state by state: low-pass denoise → wrap to greyscale → 25% overlapping patches → detect → dilate/smooth → reassemble; slopes >5° and the earthquake catalogue exclude landslides and quakes. p.4
  5. 暴露与风险分析：叠加土地覆盖、耕地类型、人口、建筑与道路；以约 3 年的差异沉降（角变形）与人口密度构建风险矩阵（Text S4）。Exposure and risk: Overlay land cover, cropland type, population, buildings and roads; risk matrix from ~3-year differential settlement (angular distortion) and population density (Text S4). p.8–9
  6. 计算实现：8 台 Ubuntu 20.04 工作站（各 32 核、64 GB 内存），MATLAB/GAMMA 自编流程；每 frame 处理数据超过 1.2 TB。Computation: Eight Ubuntu 20.04 workstations (32 cores, 64 GB each), custom MATLAB/GAMMA pipeline; >1.2 TB processed data per frame. p.5

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

精度与方法检验Accuracy and method checks

  • 4242 个 GNSS 站：87.5% 速率差在 8 mm/yr 以内，6.8% 超过 1 cm/yr，RMSE 5.2 mm/yr。4242 GNSS stations: 87.5% within 8 mm/yr, 6.8% exceed 1 cm/yr, RMSE 5.2 mm/yr. p.5
  • EDAD 方法比较（P137 F123，掩膜大形变区后）：方法 1、2、4 速率标准差分别 11、12、8 mm/yr；方法 3（多块拟合）去除了大部分沉降信号。EDAD comparison (P137 F123, with large deformation masked): SDs of 11, 12 and 8 mm/yr for methods 1, 2 and 4; method 3 (multi-patch) removed most subsidence signal. p.13–14
  • 拼接前后各 frame 平均速率相对 0 的 SD 由 2.9 降至 1.4 mm/yr，MAD 由 2.1 降至 0.7 mm/yr。Per-frame mean rates relative to zero: SD from 2.9 to 1.4 mm/yr and MAD from 2.1 to 0.7 mm/yr after mosaicking. p.14
  • 测试区识别 701 个潜在沉降区，经 Google Earth 目视判读 91 个为误判，准确率约 87%；误判像元约占沉降像元 1%。At the test site 701 potential areas were detected, 91 judged false by visual interpretation (~87% accuracy); false pixels ~1% of LS pixels. p.14–15

全国沉降格局National pattern

  • 48 州与 DC 均有沉降；沉降区平均与中位速率 −4.3 与 −2.7 cm/yr；10.2% 在 −1～0、20.5% 在 −2～−1、21.1% 在 −3～−2 cm/yr；总面积约 45,666 km²，62.5% 在加州。All 48 states and DC; mean and median −4.3 and −2.7 cm/yr; 10.2% in −1–0, 20.5% in −2–−1, 21.1% in −3–−2 cm/yr; total ~45,666 km², 62.5% in California. p.5–6
  • 最大 LOS 沉降 47.5 cm/yr（中央谷地，垂向约 58 cm/yr）；LOS 速率四分位：25% <1.6、50% <2.7、75% <5.4 cm/yr；加州 24% 在 2.7–5.4、38% >5.4 cm/yr。Max LOS 47.5 cm/yr (Central Valley, ~58 cm/yr vertical); LOS quartiles 1.6, 2.7, 5.4 cm/yr; in California 24% in 2.7–5.4 and 38% >5.4 cm/yr. p.6
  • 作者列出四点可能低估：洛杉矶与圣安娜盆地在观测期不活跃；小面积或少像元区难与噪声区分；速率过小难以识别；陡坡采矿沉降被坡度筛除。Four reasons for likely underestimation: LA and Santa Ana basins inactive in the period; small or few-pixel areas indistinguishable from noise; very small rates not identified; steep-slope mining subsidence excluded by the slope filter. p.6

土地覆盖与耕地Land cover and cropland

  • 沉降面积中耕地 73%、湿地 10%、草地 5%、人工地表 4%、灌木与森林各 3%；33 个州以耕地为最大类；阿肯色、伊利诺伊、艾奥瓦、堪萨斯、俄亥俄、俄克拉何马 >90% 在农业区；佛罗里达、路易斯安那等以湿地为主；怀俄明以人工地表为主。Cultivated 73%, wetlands 10%, grassland 5%, artificial 4%, shrubs and forest 3% each; cultivated land largest in 33 states; >90% agricultural in Arkansas, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Ohio, Oklahoma; wetlands dominate in Florida, Louisiana and others; artificial surfaces dominate only in Wyoming. p.6–7
  • 灌溉耕地占耕地面积 24%，却贡献耕地沉降的 67%；仅 10 个州灌溉耕地多于雨养耕地；CA_LS1 中耕地占 84.56%。Irrigated cropland is 24% of cropland but 67% of cropland subsidence; only ten states have more irrigated than rainfed cropland; CA_LS1 is 84.56% cropland. p.8

暴露与风险Exposure and risk

  • 暴露人口约 626 万（加州 96%；亚利桑那 87,099、路易斯安那 28,429）；31 个州暴露人口超过 1000。~6.26 million exposed (California 96%; Arizona 87,099; Louisiana 28,429); 31 states exceed 1000 exposed people. p.8–9
  • 极低—低风险区占沉降面积 72.9%，高—极高风险区仅 1.6% 但涉及 9.6% 暴露人口；极高风险 278,769 人、高风险 321,266 人、中风险约 540 万人。Very low–low risk covers 72.9% of subsiding area; high–very high only 1.6% but 9.6% of exposed people; 278,769 in very high, 321,266 in high, ~5.4 million in medium risk. p.9
  • 建筑 160,071 座（加州 150,458）；道路：铁路 1753 km、高速 1779 km、一级 1425 km、二级 2766 km、干线 692 km。Buildings 160,071 (California 150,458); roads: railways 1753 km, motorways 1779, primary 1425, secondary 2766, trunk 692 km. p.10

驱动与治理案例Drivers and management cases

  • 91% 沉降区位于含水层范围内；48,341 口井中，沉降区地表—地下水埋深变化率（中位 11、均值 24 cm/yr）显著高于非沉降区（2、7 cm/yr）。91% of LS areas lie within aquifers; among 48,341 wells, depth-to-water change is higher in LS areas (median 11, mean 24 cm/yr) than elsewhere (2, 7 cm/yr). p.10
  • 加州许多沉降区与油气田重合，与地下水开采的贡献难以区分；作者强调这些案例仅示范数据用途，不确立因果机制。Many California LS zones coincide with oil and gas fields, making contributions hard to separate; the authors stress the cases only demonstrate data use and do not establish causal mechanisms. p.10
  • CA_LS1 的 GNSS 站显示：无沉降 4 站、持续沉降 16 站、沉降减缓 11 站；作者认为减缓与干湿年相关，不能归因于 SGMA；圣安娜盆地 2015-11～2024-08 转为长期抬升。GNSS in CA_LS1: 4 no subsidence, 16 continuous, 11 reduced; the slowdown is linked to wet/dry years rather than SGMA; the Santa Ana Basin shows long-term uplift for Nov 2015–Aug 2024. p.11

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors

  • 形变以局部参考点为基准，构造、GIA 等长波信号未被保留。Results are referenced to local points, so long-wavelength signals (tectonics, GIA) are not preserved. p.16
  • 形变时序只针对特定区域计算，限制对沉降动态的认识。Time series were computed only for specific areas, limiting insight into dynamics. p.16
  • 识别的沉降区很可能低估实际范围（四点原因见结果）。Identified LS areas likely underestimate the true extent (four reasons in Results). p.6
  • 风险分析的角变形仅基于约 3 年观测，累计变形可能大数十倍，实际风险可能更严重。Angular distortion reflects only ~3 years, so cumulative distortion could be dozens of times larger and actual risk more severe. p.9–10
  • 案例分析不确立因果机制，需要现场调查与模型；全国尺度验证所有沉降区具有挑战性。Case studies do not establish causality, which needs field investigation and modelling; validating all LS areas nationally is challenging. p.10, 15
  • 与 Ohenhen 等研究在暴露人口与面积上的差异，归因于 SAR 时段、参考框架与识别方法不同。Differences from Ohenhen et al. in exposed population and area are attributed to SAR periods, reference frames and identification methods. p.15–16

7.2 依据原文可核查的问题7.2 Issues verifiable in the text

每条均可在所注页码核对；只陈述事实，不推测影响。Each item can be checked on the cited page; facts only, no speculation.

  • CA_LS1 的 GNSS 站数不一致：正文称分析 27 个站，分类为 4 + 16 + 11 = 31 个。GNSS count in CA_LS1 is inconsistent: 27 stations analysed, but the categories sum to 4 + 16 + 11 = 31. p.11
  • 全部 InSAR 结果为 LOS 方向（仅升轨），文中“沉降速率”与面积统计均基于 LOS；仅最大值给出了约 58 cm/yr 的垂向换算。All InSAR results are LOS (ascending only); subsidence rates and area statistics are LOS-based, with only the maximum converted to ~58 cm/yr vertical. p.4, 6
  • DeforNet 的 F1=0.96 来自中国训练数据的 80/20 划分；美国的准确率（约 87%）只在一个测试 frame（P137 F123）上以 Google Earth 目视判读得到。DeforNet’s F1=0.96 comes from the 80/20 split of Chinese training data; US accuracy (~87%) was obtained in one test frame (P137 F123) by visual interpretation in Google Earth. p.4, 14
  • 摘要与结论称地下水超采是“predominant / primary driver”，而 4.1 节明确说明相关案例“do not establish definitive causal mechanisms”。The abstract and conclusion call groundwater over-exploitation the “predominant/primary driver”, while Section 4.1 states the cases “do not establish definitive causal mechanisms”. p.1, 10, 16
  • 拼接以“每 frame 常数偏差”建模，且只用 |速率|<1 cm/yr 的像元估计偏差。Mosaicking models a constant bias per frame and estimates it only from pixels with |rate|<1 cm/yr. p.4
  • 代码仅“可应合理请求提供”；形变图需在 insarclub 网站注册免费账户获取。Code is available only upon reasonable request; maps require a free account on the insarclub website. p.16
