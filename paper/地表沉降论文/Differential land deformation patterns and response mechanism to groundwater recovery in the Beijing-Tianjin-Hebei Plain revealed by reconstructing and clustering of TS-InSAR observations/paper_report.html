一句话概括（依据摘要）：对 2016–2024 年三条 Sentinel-1A 轨道的 TS-InSAR 形变场拼接后，分别重建季节项与长期项，用长期形变特征约束的 K-means 将京津冀平原分为四类形变模式，并结合地下水位分析年周期滞后与长期延迟压密。One-line summary (from the abstract): After mosaicking 2016–2024 TS-InSAR deformation from three Sentinel-1A paths, the study reconstructs seasonal and long-term components, classifies the BTH Plain into four deformation patterns with a long-term-feature-constrained K-means, and combines groundwater levels to separate annual lag from long-term delayed compaction. p.1

2. 研究区域2. Study area

  • 京津冀（BTH）平原，华北平原主体，毗邻燕山、太行山和渤海；覆盖北京、天津、河北的平原区，面积约 87,836.67 km²。The Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei (BTH) Plain, the main part of the North China Plain, bordered by the Yanshan and Taihang Mountains and the Bohai Sea; it covers the plain areas of Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei, about 87,836.67 km². p.2
  • 地貌上从山前到沿海分为山前平原（PP）、中部平原（CP）、东部平原（EP）；第四系松散沉积形成的孔隙含水层系统厚度超过 100 m。Geomorphically divided from mountain front to coast into piedmont plain (PP), central plain (CP) and eastern plain (EP); the Quaternary porous aquifer system is more than 100 m thick. p.2
  • PP 以单层砂性含水层为主；多层结构（砂黏互层）分布在各亚平原底部；从 PP 到 EP 含水层变薄、粒度变细、渗透性变差。Single-layer sandy aquifers dominate the PP; multi-layer sand–clay structures occur at the bottom of each sub-plain; from PP to EP the aquifer thins, grain size decreases and permeability worsens. p.2
  • 平均年降水约 550 mm，汛期降水占全年 75% 以上，年际变化显著。Mean annual precipitation is about 550 mm, with over 75% in the flood season and marked interannual variability. p.2
  • 背景：南水北调中线 2014 年正式通水，截至 2025 年 2 月累计调水超过 700 亿 m³；北京、天津地下水位自 2016 年以来明显回升，河北部分地区因农业灌溉需求超采尚未完全扭转。Context: the SNWDP Middle Route started in 2014 and had delivered more than 70 billion m³ by February 2025; groundwater levels in Beijing and Tianjin have recovered notably since 2016, while over-exploitation in parts of Hebei driven by irrigation has not been fully reversed. p.1–2

4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. IPTA 时序 InSAR：三轨影像沿方位向拼接；增强谱分集配准，方位向偏移 <0.001 像元；方位向去斜；相邻三景组网；距离×方位多视 10×2；SRTM 去地形；64/32/16 像元自适应迭代滤波；最小费用流解缠；平均相干 >0.6、标准差 <0.3、强度阈值 0.01 选点；SVD 求时序；迭代估计并去除大气和残余地形。IPTA time-series InSAR: Paths concatenated along azimuth; enhanced spectral diversity co-registration (<0.001 pixel); azimuth de-ramping; three-adjacent-scene network; 10×2 multi-looking; SRTM topographic removal; iterative adaptive filtering (64/32/16 px); minimum cost flow unwrapping; pixels with mean coherence >0.6, std <0.3, intensity ≥0.01; SVD time-series inversion; iterative removal of atmosphere and residual topography. p.4
  2. 轨间校正与拼接：假定水平形变可忽略，用 d_ver = d_los/cosθ 转垂向；以经水准验证的 path 142 为参考轨；在重叠区 0.5 像元内匹配同名像元，构建双层 Delaunay 三角网检验局部梯度一致性；以同名像元垂向形变差的中位数作为基准偏移，逐月改正 path 40 与 path 69。Inter-path correction and mosaicking: Horizontal motion assumed negligible, d_ver = d_los/cosθ; leveling-validated path 142 as reference; homonymous pixels matched within 0.5 pixel in overlaps; double-layer Delaunay triangulation to test gradient consistency; the median vertical difference used as datum offset and applied month by month to paths 40 and 69. p.4
  3. 季节项与长期项重建：季节项只取年周期谐波 Def(t)=A·cos(2πt)+B·sin(2πt)，年振幅 A_def=√(A²+B²)；长期项用最小二乘二次多项式 at²+bt+c，并派生转折时间 t_vertex=−b/(2a) 与末期斜率 slope_end。Seasonal and long-term reconstruction: Seasonal term uses only the annual harmonic Def(t)=A·cos(2πt)+B·sin(2πt), amplitude A_def=√(A²+B²); long-term term is a least-squares quadratic at²+bt+c, with derived turning time t_vertex=−b/(2a) and terminal slope slope_end. p.5
  4. 特征约束 K-means：每个点用 (a, b, t_vertex, slope_end) 四维特征，z-score 标准化，欧氏距离，随机初始质心迭代至收敛；肘部法确定 K=4；并与直接对原始时序聚类的结果比较（补充材料 S3）。Characteristic-constrained K-means: Each point represented by (a, b, t_vertex, slope_end), z-score normalised, Euclidean distance, random initial centroids iterated to convergence; elbow method gives K=4; compared with clustering the raw time series (Supplement S3). p.5–6, 8
  5. 不确定性评估：用 RMSE 与按累计形变归一化的 NRMSE 评价重建误差。Uncertainty assessment: RMSE and NRMSE (normalised by cumulative deformation) assess reconstruction error. p.11
  6. 滞后分析：STFT：对代表井的去趋势形变与 GWL 做短时傅里叶变换（交叉功率谱，95% 置信），并用相位差求年周期滞后。Lag analysis: STFT: Short-time Fourier transform (cross-power spectra, 95% confidence) of detrended deformation and GWL at representative wells; phase difference gives annual lag. p.12–13
  7. 长期延迟压密：指数衰减：对 cluster-3/4 的长期趋势拟合时间相关指数衰减模型（式 11），用衰减系数 K 比较延迟压密程度，并与断层分布对比。Long-term delayed compaction: exponential decay: A time-dependent exponential decay model (Eq. 11) is fitted to the long-term trends of clusters 3 and 4; the decay coefficient K is compared spatially and with fault locations. p.14

5. 创新点（作者自述）5. Innovations (author claims)

以下为作者在文中声称的贡献/新意，本笔记不对其作额外评价。Contributions claimed by the authors; no extra assessment is added.

  • 把三轨 TS-InSAR 形变场校正、拼接为覆盖整个京津冀平原的连续形变场，而非只针对单个城市或局部（作者指出既有研究多聚焦典型城市或局部）。Corrects and mosaics three-path TS-InSAR into a continuous deformation field over the whole BTH Plain, whereas existing studies mostly focus on single cities or local areas (as the authors note). p.2
  • 提出“长期形变特征约束的 K-means”：以具有物理意义的二次曲线参数代替高维原始时序进行聚类，以减少短期噪声影响、提高可解释性。Proposes a long-term-characteristic-constrained K-means that clusters physically interpretable quadratic parameters instead of high-dimensional raw time series, to reduce short-term noise and improve interpretability. p.2, 5–6
  • 在同一框架内区分形变对地下水变化的“年周期滞后”（STFT）与“长期延迟响应”（指数衰减）。Distinguishes, in one framework, the annual lag (STFT) and the long-term delayed response (exponential decay) of deformation to GWL change. p.1, 15
  • 作者自述建立了“TS-InSAR 形变重建 + 形变模式识别 + 地下水—形变响应分析”的综合框架。The authors state that they establish an integrated framework combining TS-InSAR reconstruction, pattern recognition and groundwater–deformation response analysis. p.15

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

形变速率与验证Deformation rates and validation

  • path 142 与水准对比，多数点误差在 ±10 mm 内；path 69 经基准改正后与水准一致性显著提高；改正后与相邻轨的月度差异 RMSE 均降低（补充材料 S5）。Against leveling, most path-142 errors fall within ±10 mm; after datum correction, path 69 agrees much better with leveling; monthly RMSE between adjacent paths decreases after correction (Supplement S5). p.6
  • 以 2018 年为界分 2016–2018 与 2018–2024 两期：显著沉降（≥40 mm/yr）主要在中部与东部平原，并与历史承压水漏斗吻合。Two periods split at 2018 (2016–2018, 2018–2024): significant subsidence (≥40 mm/yr) is mainly in the central and eastern plains and coincides with historical confined-water funnels. p.6
  • 形变速率 0.1%–99.9% 分位区间由 −112.1～28.0 mm/yr（2016–2018）收窄为 −98.4～26.5 mm/yr（2018–2024）。The 0.1%–99.9% range of deformation rate narrows from −112.1–28.0 mm/yr (2016–2018) to −98.4–26.5 mm/yr (2018–2024). p.6

季节形变Seasonal deformation

  • 年振幅 A_def 为 0–15 mm；山前区一般 <5 mm，中部与东部平原及主要沉降漏斗明显更大。Annual amplitude A_def is 0–15 mm; generally <5 mm in the piedmont, markedly larger in the central and eastern plains and major funnels. p.7
  • 深层承压井水位季节振幅普遍大于浅层井，邢台、衡水漏斗尤为明显。Seasonal GWL amplitudes in deep confined wells generally exceed those in shallow wells, especially around the Xingtai and Hengshui funnels. p.8

四类长期形变模式Four long-term deformation patterns

  • 肘部法给出 K=4。cluster-1（24.91%）：a 小、b 近零或弱负、slope_end 多为正，稳定到轻微抬升，主要在西部与北部山前。Elbow method gives K=4. Cluster-1 (24.91%): small a, near-zero or weakly negative b, mostly positive slope_end; stable to slightly uplifting; mainly in the western and northern piedmont. p.8
  • cluster-2（46.09%）：形变量较小，slope_end 集中于 0 附近，t_vertex 中等，监测末期总体趋稳（原文未给出其具体分布地名）。Cluster-2 (46.09%): small magnitudes, slope_end centred near zero, moderate t_vertex, generally stabilising by the end (the paper names no specific locations for it). p.8
  • cluster-3（18.86%）与 cluster-4（10.14%）为沉降最严重区，位于主要漏斗内及周边；cluster-4 的 slope_end 全为负；cluster-3 的 slope_end 从弱负到正，分布于北京、天津西部、衡水东部、邯郸北部。Cluster-3 (18.86%) and cluster-4 (10.14%) are the most severely subsiding areas, in and around major funnels; cluster-4 has entirely negative slope_end; cluster-3 ranges from weakly negative to positive and lies in Beijing, western Tianjin, eastern Hengshui and northern Handan. p.8
  • 与原始时序聚类相比，特征约束聚类空间连续性更好；原始时序聚类在主要漏斗内常出现多个类别（补充材料 S3）。Compared with clustering raw time series, the constrained clustering is spatially more continuous; raw-series clustering often places several clusters within major funnels (Supplement S3). p.8–9
  • cluster-4 与不同深度的水位下降漏斗对应：保定—衡水与邯郸主要对应浅层承压水下降；廊坊与邢台主要对应深层；唐山沿海对应浅、深层同时下降。Cluster-4 matches GWL decline cones at different depths: Baoding–Hengshui and Handan mainly shallow confined; Langfang and Xingtai mainly deep confined; coastal Tangshan both. p.9
  • 代表井（图 11 共 11 口）：cluster-1 的 A、B 井相关系数普遍 >0.65；cluster-2 的 C 井与 cluster-3 的 D、E 井 2023 年后沉降速率下降，部分反弹；cluster-4 的 F、G、H 井形变与水位相关性低；K 井持续沉降与水位下降强正相关。Representative wells (11 in Fig. 11): cluster-1 wells A and B generally r >0.65; cluster-2 well C and cluster-3 wells D, E slow down after 2023, partly rebounding; cluster-4 wells F, G, H show low correlation with GWL; well K shows strong positive correlation between continuous subsidence and declining GWL. p.9

重建不确定性与用水结构Reconstruction uncertainty and water-use structure

  • RMSE 在山前小、中东部漏斗大；cluster-3/4 典型漏斗 NRMSE 均低于 6%；cluster-1/2 含部分山前与沿海区，噪声占比较高。RMSE is small in the piedmont and large in central–eastern funnels; NRMSE in typical cluster-3/4 funnels is all below 6%; clusters 1/2 include piedmont and coastal parts with a higher noise share. p.11
  • 按 slope_end 是否高于 cluster-3 上四分位数，解释性地细分为 3a（沉降转反弹）与 3b（沉降趋停）；该细分不改变原聚类。3a 主要在天津（亦见于沧州、衡水），北京沉降区以 3b 为主。Cluster-3 is split for interpretation by whether slope_end exceeds its upper quartile: 3a (subsidence to rebound) and 3b (subsidence stopping); this does not change the clustering. 3a is mainly in Tianjin (also Cangzhou, Hengshui); Beijing’s subsiding area is mainly 3b. p.12
  • 北京、天津地下水供水比例自 2008 年以来持续下降，天津最低；cluster-4 所在的保定、邢台、邯郸、唐山在 2020 年前后地下水供水比例仍相对较高。Groundwater-supply proportions in Beijing and Tianjin have declined since 2008, Tianjin being lowest; cluster-4 cities (Baoding, Xingtai, Handan, Tangshan) still had relatively high proportions around 2020. p.12

周期与滞后响应Periodic and lagged response

  • STFT 显示形变与水位的显著共振周期为 12–36 个月（95% 置信），作者认为是累积响应而非瞬时弹性响应。STFT shows dominant deformation–GWL resonance periods of 12–36 months (95% confidence), interpreted by the authors as a cumulative rather than instantaneous elastic response. p.13
  • 2021、2023 等异常湿润年 STFT 能量明显增强，部分代表井出现水位恢复伴随短期反弹。In anomalously wet years such as 2021 and 2023, STFT energy intensifies and several wells show GWL recovery with short-term rebound. p.13
  • cluster-1 的 A、B 井年周期滞后 <2 个月，A 井仅 0.35 个月；cluster-3/4 的 E、F、G、H 井滞后 4–6 个月。Annual lag is <2 months at cluster-1 wells A and B (0.35 months at well A) and 4–6 months at wells E, F, G, H in clusters 3/4. p.13

长期延迟压密与断层Long-term delayed compaction and faults

  • cluster-4（保定—邢台）的 K 总体低于 cluster-3（衡水）；补充材料 S4 剖面显示前者黏土层更厚更连续，后者黏土较薄并夹粉土含水层。K of cluster-4 (Baoding–Xingtai) is generally lower than cluster-3 (Hengshui); profiles in Supplement S4 show thicker, more continuous clay for the former and thinner clay interbedded with silt aquifers for the latter. p.14
  • 低 K（<0.002）并不集中在漏斗中心：廊坊、邢台、唐山沿海漏斗中低 K 集中在有断层的漏斗边缘；邢台 B–B′ 剖面中沉降速率由中心向边缘减小而 K 同步减小。Low K (<0.002) is not concentrated at funnel centres: in the Langfang, Xingtai and coastal Tangshan funnels it concentrates at fault-bearing edges; along Xingtai profile B–B′, both subsidence rate and K decrease from centre to edge. p.14
  • 无断层控制的保定—石家庄漏斗（A–A′ 剖面）低 K 集中在中心并向边缘增大。In the Baoding–Shijiazhuang funnel without fault control (profile A–A′), low K concentrates at the centre and increases toward the edges. p.14

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors
