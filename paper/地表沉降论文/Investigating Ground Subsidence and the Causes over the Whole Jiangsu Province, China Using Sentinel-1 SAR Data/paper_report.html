一句话概括（依据摘要）：用 6 个 frame 的 Sentinel-1 IW 影像（2016–2018）对全江苏省（107,200 km²）做时序 InSAR，详细介绍三级配准、平均沉降速度与时序反演及多 frame 拼接；对 9,276,214 个相干点给出结果，水准评估精度 3.9 mm/yr；结合历史光学影像和野外调查分析成因。One-line summary (from the abstract): Time-series InSAR with six Sentinel-1 IW frames (2016–2018) over the whole of Jiangsu (107,200 km²), detailing three-level co-registration, mean subsidence velocity and time-series retrieval and multi-frame mosaicking; results for 9,276,214 coherent pixels with 3.9 mm/yr accuracy against leveling; causes analysed with historical optical images and fieldwork. p.1

2. 研究区域2. Study area

  • 江苏全省，107,200 km²，位于中国东部沿海中部、长江三角洲地区；80% 以上为平原，海拔低于 50 m；共 13 个地级市。The whole of Jiangsu Province, 107,200 km², central eastern coast in the Yangtze River Delta region; over 80% plain below 50 m; 13 prefectures. p.1, 3
  • 背景：1980 年代以来地面沉降逐步发展；至 2000 年苏锡常地区 5000 km² 累计沉降超过 0.2 m，最大 2 m；2014 年省政府发布十年防治规划。Background: subsidence developed since the 1980s; by 2000, 5000 km² of the Suzhou–Wuxi–Changzhou area exceeded 0.2 m cumulative subsidence, max 2 m; a 10-year plan was issued in 2014. p.1–2

4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. GDEMSI / MCTSB-InSAR：使用中国测绘科学研究院自研软件 GDEMSI，基于多主影像相干目标小基线 InSAR（MCTSB-InSAR）。GDEMSI / MCTSB-InSAR: In-house GDEMSI software (CASM) based on Multiple-master Coherent Target Small-Baseline InSAR (MCTSB-InSAR). p.4
  2. 三级粗到精配准：① 由轨道与 SRTM 生成查找表（约 0.5 像元）；② 振幅互相关测偏移并线性拟合修正查找表；③ ESD 利用 burst 重叠区相位差，把方位残差降至千分之一像元；多干涉对加权最小二乘联合求解。Three-level coarse-to-fine co-registration: (1) Look-up table from orbits and SRTM (~0.5 pixel); (2) amplitude cross-correlation offsets with linear refinement; (3) ESD from burst-overlap phase to reach 1/1000 pixel; joint weighted least squares over multiple pairs. p.4
  3. 干涉图筛选：夏季（6–9 月）影像组成的干涉图受强对流层扰动，被剔除；未连入网络的影像也被弃用。Interferogram screening: Interferograms formed from summer (June–September) images were heavily affected by troposphere and discarded, together with unconnected acquisitions. p.5–6
  4. 垂向化与拼接：假设只有垂向形变（引文献称江苏以沉降/抬升为主），LOS 速率除以 cosθ 得平均沉降速度（MSV）；6 个 frame 平差后拼接；残余相位时空滤波得非线性形变，再投影为垂向时序。Vertical projection and mosaicking: Assuming vertical-only motion (citing that subsidence/uplift dominate in Jiangsu), LOS rates divided by cosθ give MSV; six frames adjusted and mosaicked; nonlinear deformation from spatio-temporal filtering of residual phase, projected to vertical series. p.6
  5. 精度评估：每个水准点取 80 m 内最近 CP，按水准起止日期从时序中提取速率并比较。Accuracy evaluation: For each leveling point the nearest CP within 80 m is used; its rate over the leveling period is extracted and compared. p.8
  6. 成因分析：对五个重点沉降区结合 CP 时序、历史光学影像与野外调查推断成因；采矿区另用 10–3 月相干期的 12/24 天干涉图最小二乘求累计形变。Cause analysis: Causes in five zones inferred from CP series, historical optical images and fieldwork; in the mining area, 12/24-day interferograms in coherent October–March periods are least-squares combined. p.9–18

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

全省形变Province-wide deformation

  • 约 82% 的 CP 的 MSV 在 [−5, 5] mm/yr；1,319,077 个（14.22%）在 [−30, −5]；30,975 个（0.33%）在 [−50, −30]；7934 个（0.086%）在 [−215, −50] mm/yr。About 82% of CPs have MSV in [−5, 5]; 1,319,077 (14.22%) in [−30, −5]; 30,975 (0.33%) in [−50, −30]; 7934 (0.086%) in [−215, −50] mm/yr. p.6–7
  • 313,005 个 CP（3.37%）抬升 [5, 32] mm/yr，其中 80% 在 [5, 10]；三处抬升集中区原因不明，淮安内陆可能与地下水位回升有关，盐城沿海两处可能为潮汐或大气误差。313,005 CPs (3.37%) show uplift [5, 32] mm/yr, 80% within [5, 10]; causes of three uplift zones unclear—possibly rising groundwater inland (Huai’an), tides or atmospheric errors on the Yancheng coast. p.7
  • 水准对比：常州 237 点 SD 4.0、南通 223 点 4.0、淮安两期 22 点 3.4 与 45 点 3.3，总体 527 点 3.9 mm/yr。Leveling: Changzhou 237 points SD 4.0, Nantong 223 points 4.0, Huai’an 22 points 3.4 and 45 points 3.3; overall 527 points 3.9 mm/yr. p.8

五个重点沉降区Five major zones

  • A 区（连云港港区码头路，填海）：BC 段 2014-10～2016-01 新填，MSV [−215, −100]；AB 段 2014-10 前完成，[−80, −8]；2018-09-18 后 P1 减缓、P2 小幅抬升；作者按 Terzaghi 固结理论推断 BC 处于主固结后期、AB 处于次压缩阶段。Zone A (Lianyungang dock road, reclamation): BC reclaimed Oct 2014–Jan 2016, MSV [−215, −100]; AB completed before Oct 2014, [−80, −8]; after 18 Sep 2018 P1 slows and P2 slightly rises; by Terzaghi theory BC is in late primary consolidation and AB in secondary compression. p.9
  • B 区（徐州西北）：丰县城区水井密集，MSV [−89, −20]，与井分布空间相关，作者推断为地下水开采；煤矿区农田缺少 CP，相干期干涉图显示 WP 92,606 首个相干期累计形变达 670 mm。Zone B (NW Xuzhou): Feng county urban area with dense wells, MSV [−89, −20], spatially related to wells and attributed to groundwater withdrawal; farmland mining area lacks CPs, but coherent-period interferograms show 670 mm cumulative deformation at WP 92,606. p.9, 13
  • 采矿形变强烈非线性，时序 InSAR 结果（P1）明显小于相干期最小二乘结果（P1*），作者认为采矿区沉降在空间上漏测、量值上被低估。Mining deformation is strongly nonlinear; the time-series result (P1) is clearly smaller than the coherent-period estimate (P1*), so mining subsidence is spatially missed and underestimated. p.13
  • C 区（盐城中南部沿海）：2003 年水田与泥滩 2017 年变为丰源热电厂与盐城钢铁集团，MSV [−139, −21]；作者认为软土层固结肯定是原因，地下水开采可能也是（野外未发现抽水证据）。Zone C (central-south Yancheng coast): paddy and mudflat (2003) became the Fengyuan power plant and Yancheng Steel Group (2017), MSV [−139, −21]; consolidation of soft layers is certainly a cause, groundwater withdrawal possibly (no pumping evidence found in the field). p.14–15
  • D 区（南通如东沿海）：沿海沉降带 MSV [−31, −20]；紫菜养殖清洗用水塔抽水与生活井；时序 1–9 月沉降、9 月至次年 1 月抬升；2018 年末回弹减小，据当地居民称当年关停部分养殖点。Zone D (Rudong coast, Nantong): coastal belt MSV [−31, −20]; water towers pumping for laver cleaning plus domestic wells; subsidence Jan–Sep, uplift Sep–Jan; reduced rebound in late 2018, when residents said some laver sites were closed. p.16
  • E 区（苏州吴江，太湖南岸）：农田、水田与鱼塘 2017 年转为住宅；P1（塘→建筑）−41 mm/yr，P2（农田→建筑，早至少 3 年）−15 mm/yr；作者推断为软土固结，区内无过量开采地下水。Zone E (Wujiang, Suzhou, south of Taihu): farmland/paddy/ponds converted to housing by 2017; P1 (pond→building) −41 mm/yr, P2 (farmland→building, ≥3 years earlier) −15 mm/yr; attributed to soft-soil consolidation with no excessive pumping. p.17–18
  • 作者归纳三类情形：软土固结（A、E 区）、地下水开采（B、D 区）、地下采矿（B 区煤矿）。Three scenarios summarised: soft-soil consolidation (Zones A, E), groundwater withdrawal (Zones B, D), underground mining (Zone B). p.18–19

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors

  • 徐州采矿区农田因地表覆盖频繁变化几乎无 CP，沉降在空间上漏测；采矿形变强烈非线性，线性相位模型/时序分析会低估量值。In the Xuzhou mining area, frequent land-cover change leaves almost no CPs, so subsidence is spatially missed; strongly nonlinear mining deformation is underestimated by time-series analysis. p.13, 19
  • 抬升区成因不清：内陆可能为地下水位回升，沿海可能为潮汐波动或大气伪影误差。Causes of uplift are unclear: possibly rising groundwater inland, tides or atmospheric errors on the coast. p.7
  • 夏季干涉图受强对流层影响被剔除，因此实际使用影像少于存档数量。Summer interferograms were discarded due to strong troposphere, so fewer images were used than archived. p.5–6
  • 水准测量多由地级市组织，并非每个市都有，点密度、频次和起止时间各异。Leveling campaigns are run by prefectures, not all of which survey; point density, frequency and dates vary. p.8
  • C 区野外调查未发现抽取地下水的证据，地下水开采仅被列为“可能”原因。No evidence of groundwater pumping was found in Zone C; withdrawal is only a “possible” contributor. p.15

7.2 依据原文可核查的问题7.2 Issues verifiable in the text

每条均可在所注页码核对；只陈述事实，不推测影响。Each item can be checked on the cited page; facts only, no speculation.

  • 摘要称“用 688 个水准测量评估，MSV 精度 3.9 mm/yr”，正文显示实际使用 527 个点，且 3.9 mm/yr 为差值标准差。The abstract says 688 leveling measurements gave 3.9 mm/yr accuracy, but the text shows 527 points were used and 3.9 mm/yr is the standard deviation of differences. p.1, 8
  • 结论称“约 82% 的国土（territory）稳定”，结果部分的 82% 是相干点比例；文中同时说明农田等区域缺少 CP。The conclusion says “around 82% of the territory is stable”, but the 82% in the results refers to coherent pixels; the text also notes CPs are missing over farmland. p.6, 13, 19
  • 研究时段写为 2016–2018，但表 1 中 Frame 1、5、6 的影像始于 2015 年 11 月。The period is stated as 2016–2018, but Frames 1, 5 and 6 in Table 1 start in November 2015. p.1, 3
  • 水准验证只覆盖常州、南通、淮安三市；重点沉降区 A（连云港）、B（徐州）、C（盐城）、E（苏州）所在地级市不在验证范围内。Leveling validation covers only Changzhou, Nantong and Huai’an; the prefectures of Zones A (Lianyungang), B (Xuzhou), C (Yancheng) and E (Suzhou) are not included. p.8
  • 仅使用升轨，并以“只有垂向形变”的假设把 LOS 转为 MSV（依据为引用文献）。Only ascending data are used, with LOS converted to MSV under a vertical-only assumption justified by a cited reference. p.2–3, 6
  • 成因均为定性推断（时序形态、影像、访谈）；D 区的“约一个月滞后”假设引自北京平原的研究。Causes are inferred qualitatively (series shape, imagery, interviews); Zone D’s “approximately one-month lag” is assumed from a Beijing Plain study. p.16
  • 小问题：图 4 图注称各区在第 5 节详细讨论，实际讨论在第 4 节。Minor: the Fig. 4 caption says zones are discussed in Section 5; they are discussed in Section 4. p.7
  • 数据可应合理请求向通讯作者获取。Data available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. p.20
