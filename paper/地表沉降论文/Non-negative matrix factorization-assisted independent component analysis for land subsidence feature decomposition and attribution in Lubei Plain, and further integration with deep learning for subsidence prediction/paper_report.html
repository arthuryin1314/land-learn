2. 研究区域2. Study area

  • 鲁北平原（LBP），位于华北平原东南部；1970 年代起大规模开采地下水。西部德州、聊城以深层地下水灌溉为主（水位下降超过 100 m，2015 年后回升）；东部东营卤水广布用于制盐和养殖，并有胜利油田大规模采油。The Lubei Plain (LBP) in the south-eastern North China Plain, with large-scale pumping since the 1970s; western Dezhou and Liaocheng use deep groundwater for irrigation (decline >100 m, rebounding after 2015); eastern Dongying extracts brine for salt and aquaculture and hosts the Shengli Oilfield. p.2–3
  • 温带季风气候，年均降水约 670 mm（集中于 7–8 月），蒸发 1000–1300 mm；以黄河冲积平原为主，沿海为海积平原。Temperate monsoon climate, ~670 mm precipitation (mainly July–August), evaporation 1000–1300 mm; mainly Yellow River alluvial plain with marine plains along the coast. p.2
  • 德城剖面 800 m 内识别 8 个含水层与 9 个压缩层；东营、广饶 600 m 内 7 个含水层与 8 个压缩层；主要土地覆盖为冬小麦—夏玉米轮作农田。The Decheng profile shows 8 aquifers and 9 compressible layers within 800 m; Dongying/Guangrao 7 and 8 within 600 m; land cover mainly winter wheat–summer maize farmland. p.2–3

4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. SBAS-InSAR：HyP3 干涉处理（时间基线 36 天、空间基线 200 m）；MintPy 时序分析，ERA-5 大气改正，LOS 转垂向；以 142–116 为参考，对重叠区高相干点求全局偏移完成两 frame 拼接。SBAS-InSAR: HyP3 interferometry (36-day temporal, 200 m spatial baselines); MintPy time series with ERA-5 atmospheric correction, LOS to vertical; frame 142–116 as reference, global offsets from high-coherence overlap points to mosaic the two frames. p.4
  2. PCA 定成分数：以“最少主成分保留最多信息”确定 IC 数：前 3 个 PC 含 92.02%，前 4 个含 94.55%，取 3 个。PCA for component number: Choose the number of ICs as the fewest PCs retaining most information: 3 PCs hold 92.02%, 4 hold 94.55%; three chosen. p.4, 7
  3. NMF 定方向：先标准化使数据非负，再做 NMF；以与 IC 时序相似的 NMF 成分方向作为 IC 的方向。NMF for direction: Data standardised to be non-negative, then NMF; the direction of the similar NMF component is used as the IC direction. p.4, 6
  4. FastICA：基于负熵最大化非高斯性，先中心化与白化；O=M×S，M 为各 IC 时序，S 为空间得分。FastICA: Negentropy-based FastICA maximising non-Gaussianity after centring and whitening; O=M×S, with M the IC time series and S the spatial scores. p.4–5
  5. CNN-LSTM-Attention 预测：CNN（无池化）+ SE 通道注意力 + LSTM；Adam，70% 训练 / 30% 测试，初始学习率 0.001、500 epoch、400 epoch 后学习率×0.9；IC1、IC2 先用三次函数去趋势再预测、再加回趋势，IC3 不去趋势；预测的 IC 乘以分解矩阵重构全区沉降。CNN-LSTM-Attention prediction: CNN (no pooling) + SE channel attention + LSTM; Adam, 70/30 train/test, LR 0.001, 500 epochs, ×0.9 after 400; IC1 and IC2 detrended with cubic fits before prediction and trends added back, IC3 not detrended; predicted ICs multiplied by the decomposition matrix to rebuild regional subsidence. p.5–7
  6. 归因分析：在各 IC 高空间得分区选观测井，计算 IC 与实测累计沉降、水位的 Pearson r；结合土地覆盖、灌溉用水、降水与 Sentinel-2 影像解译。Attribution: Wells in high-score areas of each IC; Pearson r between the IC and measured cumulative subsidence and GWL; combined with land cover, irrigation use, precipitation and Sentinel-2 interpretation. p.6, 10–14

5. 创新点（作者自述）5. Innovations (author claims)

以下为作者在文中声称的贡献/新意，本笔记不对其作额外评价。Contributions claimed by the authors; no extra assessment is added.

  • 作者称针对现有分解方法“分解不完全、方向难确定”的问题，用 NMF 成分方向作为参照确定 3 个独立成分方向。Addresses incomplete decomposition and direction ambiguity by using NMF component directions to fix three IC directions. p.1
  • 在 IC 高空间得分区选井，分析 IC、形变与水位的关系，以进行归因。Selects wells in high-score areas to relate ICs, deformation and groundwater for attribution. p.1
  • 把 ICA 与 CNN-LSTM-Attention 结合，先预测各 IC 再用分解矩阵重构，实现区域沉降预测（作者称无需逐点预测）。Combines ICA with CNN-LSTM-Attention: predict each IC then reconstruct with the decomposition matrix, achieving regional prediction without per-point forecasting. p.1, 15
  • 作者称通过 IC3 方向反转，把周期性沉降进一步区分为灌溉驱动与降水驱动两类，提供新的解释思路。By reversing IC3, the periodic signal is further separated into irrigation-driven and precipitation-driven parts, offered as a new interpretive approach. p.13–15

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

模拟信号检验Synthetic test

  • 三个模拟信号混合后：PCA 分解效果差；ICA 分解精度优于 NMF，但 IC1 方向与真实信号相反；NMF 方向准确但仍含其他信号成分。With three mixed synthetic signals: PCA performs poorly; ICA is more accurate than NMF but IC1 is reversed; NMF gets directions right but still mixes signals. p.6

沉降监测与分解Monitoring and decomposition

  • 沉降中心位于东营东北沿海及聊城、德州部分地区，最大沉降 275 mm/yr；部分地区抬升最大 90 mm/yr，作者认为限采措施与南水北调等起到缓解作用。Subsidence centres in north-eastern coastal Dongying and parts of Liaocheng and Dezhou, max 275 mm/yr; uplift up to 90 mm/yr in places, attributed by the authors to extraction limits and the SNWDP. p.6
  • NMF1：趋势下降叠加周期波动，东部沿海与东南最大；NMF2：先升后降，滨州、淄博等中部；NMF3：缓慢抬升，德州南部小片区。NMF1: declining trend with periodic fluctuation, largest in the east coast and south-east; NMF2: rise then decline, central Binzhou/Zibo; NMF3: slow uplift, small area of southern Dezhou. p.6–7
  • IC1：近线性沉降，东营东北沿海与南部、聊城、德州部分；IC2：以 2020 年为拐点先升后降，滨州、淄博等中部；IC3：明显周期性，聊城、德州中西部。IC1: near-linear subsidence in NE coastal and southern Dongying and parts of Liaocheng/Dezhou; IC2: rise then decline turning in 2020, central Binzhou/Zibo; IC3: clear periodicity, central-western Liaocheng/Dezhou. p.7

预测Prediction

  • 测试集：IC1 去趋势后 RMSE 0.06、R² 0.86；IC2 RMSE 0.25、R² 0.84；IC3 RMSE 0.69、R² 0.80。Test set: IC1 (detrended) RMSE 0.06, R² 0.86; IC2 RMSE 0.25, R² 0.84; IC3 RMSE 0.69, R² 0.80. p.7
  • 重构 2023 年 1、6、12 月全区沉降：1 月与 6 月西南部误差最大，12 月西南与北部较高，作者归因于 IC3 预测精度较低。Reconstructed January, June and December 2023: errors highest in the south-west in January and June, south-west and north in December, attributed to poorer IC3 prediction. p.7

归因Attribution

  • IC1：高得分区 18 口井（4 承压、14 潜水），与沉降相关 0.80–0.97；但水位明显回升而沉降持续，作者给出三种可能：开采层与监测层不一致、石油开采、点观测局限；Sentinel-2 显示 A–C 区为农田、盐田与养殖，D 区为大型石化企业，E–G 区为农田；归因为深层承压水灌溉超采、卤水开采（养殖/制盐）及石油开采与加工，对应深部压缩层。IC1: 18 wells (4 confined, 14 unconfined), r 0.80–0.97 with subsidence; groundwater clearly rebounds while subsidence continues, explained by mismatched extraction/monitored layers, oil extraction and point-measurement limits; Sentinel-2 shows farmland, salt fields and aquaculture (A–C), a large petrochemical plant (D), farmland (E–G); attributed to deep confined pumping for irrigation, brine extraction and oil extraction/processing, in deep compressible layers. p.10–11
  • IC2：14 口井（8 承压、6 潜水），与沉降最大 0.72、与水位最大 0.56；与年际降水一致（2020 年降水最多、IC2 最大），但高得分区与高降水区不完全对应；作者归因于降水总量趋势相关的中浅层压缩层形变。IC2: 14 wells (8 confined, 6 unconfined), max r 0.72 with subsidence and 0.56 with GWL; consistent with interannual precipitation (2020 wettest and IC2 maximum), though high-score areas do not fully match high-precipitation areas; attributed to middle–shallow layers responding to precipitation totals. p.12
  • IC3：18 口井（17 潜水、1 承压），与沉降最大 0.63、与水位最大 0.71；原方向峰值多在秋冬、滞后于降水，高得分区聊城、德州灌溉用水大，归因于季节性农业灌溉（中浅层）；反转后峰值多在春夏、与降水高相关，高得分区在东南、城市用地多、灌溉少，归因于降水驱动的地表微形变。IC3: 18 wells (17 unconfined, 1 confined), max r 0.63 with subsidence and 0.71 with GWL; in the original sign peaks fall in autumn–winter, lagging precipitation, with high scores in irrigation-heavy Liaocheng/Dezhou → seasonal irrigation (middle–shallow layers); reversed, peaks fall in spring–summer, correlate with precipitation, high scores in the urbanised, less-irrigated south-east → precipitation-driven surface micro-deformation. p.12–14

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors

  • SBAS-InSAR 可靠性低于 PS-InSAR，但能获得更多监测点。SBAS-InSAR is less reliable than PS-InSAR but yields more points. p.15
  • 不同压缩层不能完全独立，跨含水层水力联系可能产生相似沉降特征，因此 IC 归因需要大量后验信息。Compressible layers are not fully independent and hydraulic connections may produce similar signals, so attribution of ICs needs much a posteriori information. p.15–16
  • 尚未按埋深与土质（砂/黏土）进一步分解。Further decomposition by burial depth and soil type (sand/clay) has not been done. p.16
  • 预测假设沉降趋势短期内不会剧烈变化（三次函数趋势与空间得分短期可用）；不使用协变量，长期预测可能误差累积。Prediction assumes trends do not change dramatically in the short term (cubic trend and spatial scores remain valid); no covariates are used, so long-term forecasts may accumulate error. p.15
  • 观测井为点观测，评价区域沉降有局限；开采层与监测层可能不一致。Wells are point measurements with limits for regional evaluation; extracted and monitored aquifers may differ. p.11
