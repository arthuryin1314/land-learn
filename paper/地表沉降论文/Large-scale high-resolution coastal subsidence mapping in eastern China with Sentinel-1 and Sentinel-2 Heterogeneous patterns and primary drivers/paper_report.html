一句话概括（依据摘要）：用 TS-InSAR 与 Sentinel-1 获取山东、江苏沿海 90 m 分辨率垂向形变，提出适用于海陆过渡带的多 frame 拼接方法；结合 Sentinel-2、沉降时序、地下水位与 PCA 分析四个典型沉降区，认为人为因素主导，且各区主要受单一因素控制。One-line summary (from the abstract): TS-InSAR with Sentinel-1 yields 90 m vertical deformation for the Shandong and Jiangsu coasts, with a multi-frame mosaicking method for land–sea transition areas; Sentinel-2, time series, groundwater levels and PCA are used to analyse four typical zones, concluding that anthropogenic factors dominate and each zone is controlled mainly by a single factor. p.1

2. 研究区域2. Study area

  • 中国东部山东与江苏两省沿海（图 1）；分析海岸带取由海岸线向陆 100 km 缓冲区（OSM 海岸线）。Coastal Shandong and Jiangsu, eastern China (Fig. 1); the coastal zone is a 100 km landward buffer from the OSM coastline. p.2, 7
  • 山东：黄河下游，中部山地隆起（泰山 1532.7 m），黄河三角洲平均高程 <10 m；大陆海岸线 3,505 km（占全国 1/6）；2023 年末常住人口超 1 亿，GDP 超 9 万亿元。Shandong: lower Yellow River, central mountains (Mount Tai 1532.7 m), Yellow River Delta <10 m; mainland coastline 3,505 km (1/6 of China); >100 million residents and GDP >9 trillion yuan at end-2023. p.2
  • 江苏：长江与淮河下游，陆地面积 107,200 km²，以平原为主；海岸线约 954 km；滨海湿地 3819 km²（约占全国 1/4）；2023 年 GDP 超 12 万亿元，常住人口 8526 万。Jiangsu: lower Yangtze and Huai rivers, 107,200 km², mostly plain; coastline ~954 km; coastal wetlands 3819 km² (~1/4 of national total); 2023 GDP >12 trillion yuan, 85.26 million residents. p.2

4. 研究方法4. Methods

点击步骤查看该步骤在原文中的具体做法。Click a step to see how the paper implements it.

  1. ISCE + MintPy SBAS：ISCE 逐景生成干涉图后拼接；多视 20×6；每景与前后各 5 景组对（每 frame >900 幅）；SNAPHU 解缠；84 m 重采样到 90 m；MintPy SBAS：相干阈值 0.7 + 最小生成树筛网、桥接与相位闭合改正解缠误差、固体潮改正、GACOS 对流层改正、残余对流层/电离层/轨道误差与 DEM 误差估计；剔除噪声影像后线性拟合求平均速率。ISCE + MintPy SBAS: ISCE interferograms per scene then stitched; 20×6 looks; each acquisition paired with five preceding and five following (>900 per frame); SNAPHU; resampled from 84 m to 90 m; MintPy SBAS: coherence 0.7 with MST network, bridging and phase-closure unwrapping corrections, solid earth tide, GACOS, residual troposphere/ionosphere/orbit and DEM error; noisy acquisitions excluded before linear velocity fit. p.3–4
  2. 垂向化：仅部分区域有升降轨；由升降轨得到的水平形变（图 S4）不显著，并引既往 GNSS/InSAR 研究，故用 dv=dLOS/cosθ 把升轨 LOS 转为垂向。Vertical conversion: Only part of the area has both geometries; horizontal motion derived where available (Fig. S4) is small and consistent with earlier GNSS/InSAR studies, so ascending LOS is converted with dv=dLOS/cosθ. p.6
  3. 沿海多 frame 拼接：基于无控制区域网平差：B 矩阵每行对应一个重叠区（+1/−1），广义加权最小二乘 + Moore–Penrose 伪逆求各 frame 改正值；观测值取重叠区速率直方图前五个频数区间中位数的平均（近似众数）；权重由两 frame 方差倒数并按有效面积与形变幅度调整；重叠区取多 frame 平均。Coastal multi-frame mosaicking: Uncontrolled block adjustment: each row of B is an overlap (+1/−1); generalised weighted least squares with the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse gives per-frame corrections; the observation is the mean of medians of the five most frequent histogram bins (mode-like); weights from inverse variance adjusted for valid area and deformation; overlaps averaged. p.4–7
  4. PCA：对典型区时序做中心化—协方差—特征分解，以各主成分方差解释率（VER）描述时间模式；作者说明 PC 数量不直接对应驱动因素数量。PCA: Centring, covariance and eigen-decomposition of zone time series; variance explained ratio (VER) describes temporal modes; the authors note PCs do not map one-to-one onto drivers. p.7
  5. 驱动分析：±10 mm/yr 视为相对稳定；选取 >20 mm/yr 的四个典型区，结合 Sentinel-2 目视解译与实地调查、土地覆盖、时序曲线和 PCA；以井周 300 m 内 InSAR 均值与地下水位线性趋势对比。Driver analysis: ±10 mm/yr treated as stable; four zones >20 mm/yr analysed with Sentinel-2 interpretation and field survey, land cover, time series and PCA; InSAR averaged within 300 m of wells compared with GWL linear trends. p.7–8, 15
  6. 精度评估：9 个 GNSS 站与最近高相干像元比较；升降轨交叉对比采用稳健统计量（中位数、MAD、NMAD、LE90）。Accuracy assessment: 9 GNSS stations vs nearest high-coherence pixel; ascending–descending cross-comparison with robust statistics (median, MAD, NMAD, LE90). p.7

6. 主要研究结果6. Main results

区域格局与精度Regional pattern and accuracy

  • 大范围沉降主要分布在山东西北部与江苏东部的第四系沉积区，山地丘陵相对稳定。Large-scale subsidence lies mainly in north-western Shandong and eastern Jiangsu on Quaternary deposits; mountains and hills are relatively stable. p.7
  • 山东西北部：原文写 47.4% 面积年均沉降速率 “less than 20 mm/year”、7.4% “less than 50”、0.9% “less than 100”；江苏沿海平原 83.1% 在 [−20, 20] mm/yr，约 16.6% 超过 20 mm/yr。NW Shandong: the text gives 47.4% of area with rates “less than 20 mm/year”, 7.4% “less than 50” and 0.9% “less than 100”; 83.1% of the Jiangsu coastal plain lies within [−20, 20] mm/yr and ~16.6% exceeds 20 mm/yr. p.7
  • 与 9 个 GNSS 站对比 RMS 1.3 mm/yr；两站观测中断；JSNT 站附近严重失相干导致解缠异常值。RMS 1.3 mm/yr against 9 GNSS stations; two stations had interruptions; severe decorrelation near JSNT caused unwrapping outliers. p.7
  • 升（P69F119）降（P76F468）轨 968,498 个重叠像元：相关 0.97，均值差 1.0、STD 6.9、RMS 7.0、中位数差 0.8、MAD 4.3、NMAD 6.3、LE90 9.8 mm/yr；其余升降轨对 STD 为 6.9、3.86、6.14 mm/yr。Ascending (P69F119) vs descending (P76F468), 968,498 pixels: r=0.97, mean diff 1.0, STD 6.9, RMS 7.0, median 0.8, MAD 4.3, NMAD 6.3, LE90 9.8 mm/yr; other pairs STD 6.9, 3.86, 6.14 mm/yr. p.7
  • 结论：沉降 >20 mm/yr 面积 23,653 km²，占研究区 15.6%；最大 344 mm/yr（黄河三角洲）。Conclusion: 23,653 km² (15.6% of the study area) subsides >20 mm/yr; maximum 344 mm/yr in the Yellow River Delta. p.16

四个典型区Four typical zones

  • A 区（东营，黄河三角洲）：最大 340 mm/yr；72.9% 面积 >20 mm/yr；>50 mm/yr 面积 370 km²（占沉降面积 39.7%）；最严重处靠近盐田与鱼塘；Z1 近线性持续沉降（2019-08～2020-03 除外），Z2 2020 年前显著沉降后趋稳；PC1 VER 94.7%/98.4%；作者归因于制盐与水产养殖抽取卤水。Zone A (Dongying, Yellow River Delta): max 340 mm/yr; 72.9% of area >20 mm/yr; 370 km² >50 mm/yr (39.7% of subsiding area); worst near salt fields and fish ponds; Z1 near-linear ongoing (except Aug 2019–Mar 2020), Z2 stabilising after 2020; PC1 VER 94.7%/98.4%; attributed to brine extraction for salt and aquaculture. p.8–9
  • B 区（莱州湾，广饶县）：最大 245 mm/yr，最大累计 161.7 cm；>20 mm/yr 面积 5031 km²（57.5%）；2019 年卤井 >1,600 口、单井约 100 m³/d、年开采 3.20×10⁷ m³（引文）；PC1 VER 99.0%/98.6%；作者归因于浅层卤水及用于灌溉、工业、生活的深层地下水开采。Zone B (Laizhou Bay, Guangrao County): max 245 mm/yr, max cumulative 161.7 cm; 5031 km² (57.5%) >20 mm/yr; >1,600 brine wells in 2019 at ~100 m³/day each, 3.20×10⁷ m³/yr (cited); PC1 VER 99.0%/98.6%; attributed to shallow brine and deep groundwater extraction for irrigation, industry and households. p.9
  • C 区（烟台，龙口港—裕龙岛）：>20 mm/yr 占 19.7%；最大 92 mm/yr、累计 52.3 cm；裕龙岛约 2011 年起填海建石化基地，作者归因于填海与工业设施荷载固结；南通的通州湾与 Maojia 港、盐城的 Shawan 港、连云港港也见类似格局。Zone C (Yantai, Longkou Port–Yulong Island): 19.7% >20 mm/yr; max 92 mm/yr, cumulative 52.3 cm; reclamation since ~2011 for a petrochemical base; attributed to reclamation and facility loading; similar patterns at Tongzhou Bay and Maojia Port (Nantong), Shawan Port (Yancheng) and Lianyungang Port. p.9–10
  • D 区（盐城王港河附近）：2017–2023 最大 138 mm/yr；>20 mm/yr 占 73.3%；最大累计 118.9 cm；约 2022 年 5 月开始显著沉降，2023 年 3 月后再变化；2022-04-02～2023-12-23 最大年速率 630 mm/yr，77.2% >50 mm/yr；变化面积 13.0 km²（占 73.4%）；PC1 VER 85.9%；作者归因于温室养殖南美白对虾大规模抽取地下水。Zone D (near the Wanggang River, Yancheng): max 138 mm/yr for 2017–2023; 73.3% >20 mm/yr; max cumulative 118.9 cm; marked subsidence from ~May 2022, changing again after March 2023; max annual rate 630 mm/yr for 2 Apr 2022–23 Dec 2023 with 77.2% >50 mm/yr; 13.0 km² changed (73.4%); PC1 VER 85.9%; attributed to groundwater extraction for greenhouse Pacific white shrimp farming. p.9–10, 14
  • 河口新生陆地、粉砂质盐沼与潮滩存在零散弱抬升，作者解释为盐沼植被截留泥沙导致快速淤积。Weak scattered uplift on new estuarine land, silty salt marshes and tidal flats, explained by rapid sediment trapping by salt-marsh vegetation. p.10

地下水与沉降Groundwater and subsidence

  • PC2、PC3 的 VER <1%，PC4 主要反映观测噪声；D 区温室池塘建设导致各 PC 同时剧烈波动。PC2 and PC3 have VER <1%, PC4 mainly noise; greenhouse-pond construction in Zone D causes simultaneous fluctuations in several PCs. p.15
  • 井周 300 m 内 InSAR 均值与水位的线性趋势变化“高度一致”；水位数据仅覆盖部分 InSAR 时段。Linear trends of InSAR (300 m around wells) and GWL are “highly consistent”; GWL covers only part of the InSAR period. p.15–16
  • 作者认为 >20 mm/yr 的显著沉降主要来自卤水与地下水等地下资源开采，与全球 55% 已发表案例一致。Significant subsidence (>20 mm/yr) is attributed mainly to extraction of brine and groundwater, consistent with 55% of published cases worldwide. p.15

7. 缺陷与局限7. Limitations and problems

7.1 作者明确承认的局限 / 假设7.1 Acknowledged by the authors

  • GNSS 对比采用最近高相干像元，对单像元噪声敏感，并假设 GNSS 与 InSAR 测量同一形变；GNSS 站通常位于相对稳定区。GNSS comparison uses the nearest high-coherence pixel, sensitive to single-pixel noise and assuming both measure the same deformation; GNSS stations are usually in stable areas. p.7
  • 严重失相干区存在解缠误差导致的离散异常值（如 JSNT 站）。Severely decorrelated areas show discrete outliers from unwrapping errors (e.g., JSNT). p.7
  • 地下水位数据只覆盖部分 InSAR 时段（2018–2021），2018 年前后站点信息存在系统偏差且有缺测。GWL covers only part of the InSAR period (2018–2021), with systematic offsets in station information before/after 2018 and gaps. p.3, 16
  • PCA 主成分数量不直接对应驱动数量，单个 PC 也不一定代表具体成因。The number of PCs does not correspond directly to the number of drivers, nor does each PC necessarily represent a specific cause. p.7
  • 仅部分区域同时有升降轨覆盖，水平形变的判断基于这些区域及既往研究。Only part of the area has both geometries; the small-horizontal-motion judgement rests on those areas and earlier studies. p.6
  • 作者说明 ICA 的前提假设在大范围、成因异质的沿海区是否成立尚不清楚（未采用 ICA 的原因）。The authors state it is unclear whether ICA assumptions hold over large, heterogeneous coastal areas (why ICA was not used). p.15
